## How AI Was Used

AI helped explain JavaScript and jQuery concepts, clarify error messages, and discuss possible solutions to implementation problems.

The team used these explanations while developing interactive features such as filtering, price calculation, form validation, and dynamic content.

AI was also consulted for Git commands, documentation, and project organization.

## Team Responsibility

The team was responsible for reviewing, testing, understanding, and integrating the project code.

Each member is expected to explain their own implementation during the final defense.

## Conclusion

AI was used as a supporting educational and development resource. The project demonstrates the team's application of HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript, and jQuery.
